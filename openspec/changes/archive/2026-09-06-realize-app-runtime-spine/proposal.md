## Why

Habitat needs a complete reusable runtime, not only cold declarations and policy.
The implemented foundation reaches deterministic compilation and boot ordering;
resource acquisition, real service binding, native hosts, process-local stop,
observation, self-hosting and released consumer adoption remain unfinished.

The September 2026 realignment preserves the semantic owner model and seven
phases, corrects cold-composition contracts, and removes an unrelated database
qualification from the generic runtime's critical path. It does not restart
from the older Rawr implementation or reduce Habitat to one consumer's wiring.

## What Changes

- Repair selected-process normalization, named service-instance dependencies,
  complete service-owned construction references, and cohesive compiler input.
  Retain deterministic identity, conflicting-diamond refusal and coldness.
- Realize the five remaining private owners for Effect provisioning, process
  runtime, harnesses, observation and mounting through complete testable stories.
- Exercise real Oclif, Elysia/oRPC, native Inngest and web boundaries, with
  process-local lifecycle, truthful cancellation/readiness and native execution.
- Complete agent/desktop authoring and web-local executable faces with real
  process execution proof; native agent/desktop hosts retain their separate D-4
  qualification and integration obligation.
- Finish the Habitat self-host and qualified command verticals without bringing
  downstream product source back into the platform.
- Distribute accepted public capability through the existing SDK/CLI release
  pair; prove installed artifacts and consumer-owned adoption separately.
- Preserve ledger, temporal inquiry and later product transfers as independently
  gated work. Their required semantics remain visible; they are not prerequisites
  for a runtime that does not consume them.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `app-runtime-realization`: meaning-preserving cold planning, provisioning,
  execution, mounting, observation, native acceptance and terminal SDK assembly.
- `repository-separation`: durable platform/product/content ownership and
  capability-specific release and migration boundaries.

### Modified Capabilities

The retained delta directories in this change own the other modified and removed
capabilities, including the three intentional full-capability removals. No delta
is applied to canonical OpenSpec merely because its artifact exists. Native
archive occurs only after the active implementation obligations are satisfied
and deferred obligations have a durable owner.

## Impact

Affected owners are the terminal SDK, private runtime owners, Habitat CLI,
qualified resources/providers/services, selected policy definitions and their
real acceptance targets. Consumers keep independent repositories and behavior
oracles. Pinned vendor mechanics remain authoritative within their boundaries;
a vendor upgrade requires renewed native proof, not copying an old receipt.

This is not authorization to publish packages, create an external Fluree fork,
restore rejected WIP, mutate hosted infrastructure, resume another task, or
delete occupied historical worktrees during preparation.
