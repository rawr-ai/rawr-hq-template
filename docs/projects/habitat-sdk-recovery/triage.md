# SDK Recovery Triage

These are unsequenced follow-ups, not an implementation plan. The
[assessment](assessment.md) owns the technical findings; this register keeps
the prerequisite investigation from being lost or mistaken for completed work.

## T-1: Recover And Validate The Collaborative Framing Method

- State: intentionally not started during consolidation, at the owner's request.
- Trigger: the owner resumes this dedicated phase after a fresh context reset.
- Source: September 15 owner clarification, captured in the
  [assessment](assessment.md#recover-the-frame-and-collaborative-method).
- Scope: verify the exact earlier Civilization 7, Magic Migration, and
  Habitat/Rawr thread identities; design the extraction and data representation;
  collect, clean, normalize, process, and analyze relevant conversation episodes
  before developing skills. Do not begin by summarizing massive raw threads.
- Preserve: chronology, human versus agent authorship, proposals versus
  agreements, corrections, keyword/bag shifts, supersession, and outcomes.
  Keep raw evidence separate from interpretations and derived guidance.
- Evaluation: recover how the frame affected decisions, including what was
  excluded or changed; test the resulting method on held-out cases rather than
  accepting a fluent skill document as evidence of usefulness.
- Review boundary: reconcile findings with the owner and update the philosophy
  and assessment before proposing SDK repairs. Existing installed skills may
  help perform the investigation; they do not substitute for this extraction.
- Data boundary: original transcript access is not authority to commit entire
  private conversation histories. Choose scoped excerpts and durable provenance
  deliberately after identifying the dataset and its sensitive content.

## T-2: Assess Repairs Through The Recovered Frame

- State: findings retained; solution selection and planning not started.
- Trigger: T-1's reviewed findings and validated skills are available, and the
  owner has reviewed the resulting philosophy and assessment revisions.
- Scope: revisit all candidate repairs in the assessment, including removal
  of unnecessary wrappers. Preserve demonstrated runtime guarantees without
  protecting incidental implementation choices from scrutiny.
- Constraint: importing an older plan or a source document's historical
  approval language does not authorize its execution today. See [D-2](deferrals.md#d-2-sdk-repair-implementation).

The independent naming operation is retained as [D-1](deferrals.md#d-1-legacy-repository-locators),
not folded into SDK or philosophy work.
