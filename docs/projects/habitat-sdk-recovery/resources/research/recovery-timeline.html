Telemetry: three different claims

Core capability does not mean full backend qualification.

GOVERNING INTENT

Native telemetry belongs in the platform

Vendor-native telemetry and meaningful lifecycle integration are part of Habitat's capability intent. The older stack is not evidence that the whole concern was abandoned.

BOUNDED DELIVERY

Provider, transport, and lifecycle

  • Telemetry provider adoption.
  • Native OTLP transmission and trace ancestry.
  • Finalization and lifecycle receipts for the qualified runtime.

RETAINED OBLIGATION · D-5

Persisted backend and event proof

  • Collector processing, storage, and record queries.
  • Secret-bearing native exception sanitization before export.
  • Semantic event integration and its outcome, correlation, and shutdown proof.

Transport receipts do not prove stored, queryable backend records or universally secret-free telemetry. Read the retained full-observability contract.
